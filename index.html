Why Victoria?

Four reasons this university stands out in New Zealand and beyond.

Top 1% worldwide

Ranked in the top 1% of the world's universities in 14 subject areas, and New Zealand's top university for research intensity.

The capital-city advantage

Pipitea campus sits beside Parliament, the courts, and the public service — law and policy students learn steps from where decisions are made.

Trimester system

Three teaching periods a year: nearly 900 courses, with more than 60 programmes welcoming new students in the July trimester.

Creative capital

Wellington is home to Weta Workshop and a thriving film and design scene — the Master of Design Technology is built with them.
