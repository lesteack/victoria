Admissions

Since 2021, achieving NCEA University Entrance guarantees entry to undergraduate study. Some programmes — Architecture, Music — add a portfolio or audition.

Coming from France?

What a French student needs for an undergraduate degree at Victoria.

Qualification

A general Baccalauréat with a 12/20 average or better (indicative) satisfies the academic requirement. No need for a New Zealand qualification.

English proof

IELTS 6.0 overall (no band below 5.5), or TOEFL, Cambridge, or PTE equivalents. Most French lycée English courses don't replace a test.

Steps

Apply online with your Bac results and transcripts → receive an offer → pay fees → apply for a student visa at Immigration New Zealand.

Try it

Pick "French Baccalauréat" in the checker above and enter your average — say, 14/20 — to see the result live during the presentation.

Good to know

Limited-entry programmes

Architecture (portfolio + interview), Music (audition), and some teacher-education programmes select beyond the academic bar.

Rank scores still matter

Guaranteed entry removed the old thresholds, but a strong rank score still helps for scholarships. Try it in the calculators.

Apply early

Trimester 1 starts in late February; international students also need a visa via Immigration NZ.
