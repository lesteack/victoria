Calculators

Three tools for the presentation — all computed in your browser, nothing sent. Official versions are linked below each.

1. NCEA rank score

Your best 80 credits at NCEA Level 3, weighted: Excellence = 4, Merit = 3, Achieved = 2. Maximum 320.

2. International fee estimator

Pick a faculty and level for an indicative tuition range, add living costs, and see the degree-level total. Official tool: wgtn.ac.nz cost calculator.

Wellington estimate: $15,000–20,000 per year

3. NZ university GPA (9-point scale)

New Zealand universities grade on a 9-point scale — enter your papers per grade to get your GPA.
